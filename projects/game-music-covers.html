Game Music Covers

I've created over 100 covers of video game songs over the years as both a hobby and a skill. I started as a longterm user of Famitracker, but eventually switched to Open ModPlug Tracker and eventually FL Studio.

My Youtube channel currently has over 1,276,000 views and 2,620 subscribers.

I prefer to let the music speak for itself, but I'll briefly add that growing up with my uncle's NES and listening to a ton of chiptune definitely gave me a unique perspective on music from a young age - one where I enjoyed reverse-engineering songs to understand their creative choices, and picking apart sounds from an intersection of music and technology. It's been a constant source of joy in my life, no matter the program or medium.
