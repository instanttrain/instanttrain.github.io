Reviews

My rubric:
"   ": Probably will not watch/play/listen again. (Not indicative of quality - I'm just picky!)
"☆": Recommended. Entertaining and/or meaningful.
"★": Outstanding. A special artistic experience. (Note: this does not signify perfection - merely the highest score I can give it.)

Kirby Star Allies

Reviewed: 8/5/2025 | Score:

Solidly built, but didn't move me much emotionally. The Friend Heart system mostly remixes ideas from other Kirby games (Super Star's Helpers, Amazing Mirror's four-Kirbys, etc.). The end result is fun gameplay that series veterans will feel like they've seen it before.

Some minor game feel issues - Friend Abilities should be a button press instead of an analog stick tilt, Friend Hearts should move at a more satisfying speed. Lore is good.

The Mystic Knights of Tir Na Nog

Reviewed: 8/20/2025 | Score:

An unintentionally hilarious blend of Saban cheese and nondescript high fantasy. Shoestring-budget television, but unintentionally funny until it starts to wear out its welcome.

Porco Rosso

Reviewed: 8/5/2025 | Score: ★

As with all Ghibli, beautifully animated. Dashing sensibilities with a lot of world-weariness. Cigarettes and coastal wine. My favorite of the Studio's films.

Ninajirachi - I Love My Computer

Reviewed: 9/3/2025 | Score: ☆

A great opportunity to stop and reflect on the Frutiger Aero 2000's and its distance to modern internet culture. A great love letter to what was.

I Am Big Bird

Reviewed: 9/12/2025 | Score:

A documentary of humility and bittersweetness (as stories that honestly talk about life are). A lovely exploration of the connection between artist and character.

The Epic of Gilgamesh (David Ferry translation)

Reviewed: 9/15/2025 | Score: ☆

Surprising just how complete it is. I don't get to read stories in verse often. A compelling meditation on change and death. I'm glad this is the oldest recorded story.

Survivor: Pearl Islands

Reviewed: 9/17/2025 | Score:

Solid television. A great cast, with a simpler, more straightforward version of Survivor. A great reminder of how important theming is for framing player mindsets.

Scooby Doo on Zombie Island

Reviewed: 9/24/2025 | Score:

Much different to watch as an adult than a child. Very well-animated by WB standards, but the inclusion of certain details and time periods adds a bit of messiness to the ethics of the whole thing.

The Three Stooges (2012)

Reviewed: 9/25/2025 | Score:

A concept probably doomed to fail from the start. Excellent effort by the leads, but way too much plot for its own good and a supporting cast that mostly doesn't bring the right rhythm for this style of gag comedy. Like watching a plane fall out of the sky, unfortunately.

17776

Reviewed: 9/25/2025 | Score: Dropped

Creative and cool, but wasn't quite able to hold my attention. No disrespect intended - I like the multimedia approach!

The Cabin in the Woods

Reviewed: 10/1/2025 | Score:

Tropey and entertaining. Very aware film that was interested in playing around with meta-knowledge for a neat gimmick. Switches to a very Sam Raimi-sort of feeling for the finale, but that's never a bad thing.

Monster House

Reviewed: 10/8/2025 | Score:

Jeez. Maybe I'm getting soft as I get older but that was a pretty harsh and intense movie by kids' standards. Not quite to my liking.

The Ring

Reviewed: 10/8/2025 | Score: ☆

Deeply impressive. The tension keeps the screws in all the way through. The color grading of this film is so insanely heavy - it clings to the audience like a wet blanket. Fantastic example of stretching a PG-13 rating as far as it can go. A cut above in nearly all categories.

Batman: Arkham City

Reviewed: 10/13/2025 | Score: ☆

Damn, this is how you make a superhero game! Intelligently made, killer combat system, and tons of fanservice in regards to lore. Surprise favorite performance: Maurice Lamarche as Calendar Man. Pretty impressive how much fun they made crisscrossing the city via gliding and grapnelling. Always a bunch of cute happenings being generated as the player travels around. A very 'playable' game, in the most literal sense.

The Blair Witch Project

Reviewed: 10/13/2025 | Score: ★

My very favorite horror movie, and a masterclass of doing a lot with a little.

The Rocky Horror Picture Show

Reviewed: 10/13/2025 | Score:

Fascinating. Must have been a great refuge for those that needed it in its era. I understand why it endures.

Pumpkinhead 2

Reviewed: 10/13/2025 | Score:

Miss. Root for the monster.

Twisted Metal (Season 1)

Reviewed: 10/27/2025 | Score:

Anthony Mackie and Stephanie Beatriz do a wonderful job. Weird, absurd, great chemistry for the leads. A well-made, stupid show that passes the Icebox Test.

Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc

Reviewed: 10/30/2025 | Score: ★

Stunning animation, but this movie actually tends to wow the most in the small, emotional moments, where Fujimoto can really dig his heels into the "millenial melancholy" that CM excels in. A rare movie that I completely feel the ticket price was worth.

The Haunted Mansion (2003)

Reviewed: 10/31/2025 | Score:

Terrence Stamp steals the show. Intended mostly as an Eddie Murphy vehicle, but is a genuinely fun time in spite of that. More fun than I remembered.

K-Pop Demon Hunters

Reviewed: 11/5/2025 | Score:

A fun time, but I wouldn't call it well-written. Very loose worldbuilding with a tendency to leave its own ideas hanging a bit limply. Second half plays tag with itself a bit and loses a lot of the joy. Quite good music, fun gags, and man, that animation. Best when it's not afraid to get "goobery" with its main cast.

House II: The Second Story

Reviewed: 11/12/2025 | Score: ?

Utterly baffling. This movie mentions and abandons ideas and tumbles down the metaphorical hill, becoming a horror film, a family adventure, and finally a western. Must be seen to be believed. John Ratzenberger steals the show.

A Doggone Christmas

Reviewed: 11/15/2025 | Score:

Delightful and hilarious, albeit unintentionally so. The premade food, the cheap costumes, and the awful acting combine to a warm and accidentally hilarious plot.

Klaus

Reviewed: 11/19/2025 | Score: ★

A movie so complete that it reminds you how incomplete others are.

Final Fantasy VIII

Reviewed: 11/20/2025 | Score:

A lot to love and a lot to hate, but that's to it's credit - this game takes big swings. Probably a few too many ideas for its own good, but a lot of credit to Square for not playing it safe after FF VII.

Kiki's Delivery Service

Reviewed: 11/22/2025 | Score: ★

Fantastic.

Castle in the Sky

Reviewed: 11/22/2025 | Score: ☆

Absolutely wild Ghibli came out of the gate with this one.

The Polar Express (2004)

Reviewed: 12/4/2025 | Score:

It has a big heart, and to its credit, Zemeckis is still Zemeckis. I just, in my gut, feel as though the best, most heartful qualities of the film probably came from the book.

Chris Porter: Ugly and Angry

Reviewed: 12/5/2025 | Score:

Not quite my brand of comedy, but it did get a few laughs out of me. There's a lot to be angry about with our modern world. Funny, relatable stuff for those with a different sense of humor.

Hundreds of Beavers

Reviewed: 12/6/2025 | Score:

They don't make movies like this anymore. Granted, they never made them like this back then, either, but still. Deranged, gory, and loaded with physical comedy. Probably could be a bit shorter than it ended up being. Quite a few video game sensibilities!

Wicked: For Good

Reviewed: 12/10/2025 | Score:

Nope. Plenty of splendor and spectacle, but this does not address the writing issues of the original play (no resolution for Boc, the world twisting to force the characters into their Wizard of Oz versions, no justification for Morrible's surrender, no real plan for Elphaba's revolution, no in-film explanation for the drought, etc.). This film was a real opportunity to rewrite the second act of the musical into something stronger and they did not take it. Very nakedly not a well-built story.

The Night Before

Reviewed: 12/12/2025 | Score:

Weak writing. Characters don't behave logically and the supporting cast is way too forgiving of everything.

Puyo Puyo Tetris 2

Reviewed: 1/17/2026 | Score:

About as good as the first game, but mostly because it doesn't introduce anything new, mostly. Story Mode is still very well-acted and charming, with a rather samey presentation. The game slides along until Chapter 4, Stage 3, when the Puyo difficulty suddenly grows muscles and slammed me through a wall.

The Martian

Reviewed: 1/21/2026 | Score: ☆

For those who love seeing competent, quick-thinking people find a way through imposing doom, this is a movie for you. A very problem-solving film. Feels similar to the emotional curve of a heist movie, honestly.

John Wick

Reviewed: 1/28/2026 | Score: ☆

A very lean, single-minded revenge movie. Being made by stunt professionals shows - all the action is ruthless and visceral. Slightly corny worldbuilding (I'm one to talk, really) smoothed over by a cast with tons of charisma and conviction.

A Fistful of Dollars

Reviewed: 2/4/2026 | Score:

Gritty, sweaty, hard-livin' Spaghetti Western stuff. Nice that the hero uses cunning as well as gunplay.

Broad City S1

Reviewed: 2/1/2026 | Score: ☆

A pretty great premise - messy, funny, authentically poor friends leave a trail of chaos behind them in a city so large, consequences only sometimes follow. More hits than misses.

Abbott Elementary S1-4

Reviewed: 2/1/2026 | Score: ★

The absolute best of the best for comedy television. Consistently funny, great cast, doesn't talk down to the audience, solid messaging. TV needs more of this right now.
